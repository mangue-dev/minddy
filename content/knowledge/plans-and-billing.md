minddy has a free plan for getting started and paid plans for larger workspaces and more AI usage. Every plan includes the MCP server and Numo. Plan limits can affect workspace capacity, AI usage, available models, and storage, so Numo should use the current account information or pricing surface rather than inventing a limit.

Users can connect a personal compatible API key and choose which AI surfaces run on it: the code agent, Numo in the app, automations, voice dictation, and feedback review. The provider bills those runs and they are not counted against minddy's included AI usage; compatible local models work too. Surfaces the key does not cover, and accounts without a key, stay on the minddy service and its plan usage.
