Projects hold a team's issues, members, categories, saved views, pages, integrations, and feedback board. An issue is one discrete piece of work. It has a title and can also carry a description, status, priority, effort, assignee, objective, categories, a due date, recurrence, linked issues, sub-issues, comments, resources, and an implementation plan.

Objectives group issues around one outcome and show their progress. Triage is the arrival area for work that needs review before it enters the normal board. Saved views filter and sort the board without changing the issues themselves. minddy uses statuses such as triage, backlog, todo, in progress, in review, done, canceled, and duplicate.
